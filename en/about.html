About

A tableon the sand.

Rouge & Roche opened in 2024 on Pointe Rouge beach, in Marseille. The restaurant is run by Thomas Blanc, who has been a restaurateur for six years.

Here, the sea is a few metres away. In summer, lunch is on the terrace, under the reeds, feet in the sand. When the wind picks up, we move to the covered terrace or indoors, behind the glass, without losing sight of the bay. That is why the restaurant stays open all year, every day, 08:00 – 22:00.

Mediterraneanand Marseille.

The menu changes every season. Right now: sole meunière, rock fish soup, octopus, panisses with rouille, marinated sardines. A dish of the day every lunchtime.

From morningto evening.

People also come for the rest of the day: breakfast facing the sea, with pastries by Mickael Martinez, a drink at sunset, the Thursday afterwork. The cellar gives pride of place to Provence wines, and the bar mixes its own cocktails.
